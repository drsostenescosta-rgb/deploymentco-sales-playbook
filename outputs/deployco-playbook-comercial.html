The constraint should not be generating more interest. It should be turning qualified demand into repeatable capacity.

Investment thesis

If paid or contracted demand exceeds measured capacity, and each additional pod delivers positive contribution, capital can convert backlog into MRR without degrading the customer experience.

Evidence gate Contracted demand, capacity per pod, cost to serve, margin, and founder dependency must be documented before the round is sized.

Strategic crux

Can Deployment.co increase recurring revenue without increasing cost, customization, and complexity at the same rate?

This is what separates a bespoke consultancy from a scalable operating system.

60-second pitch

“Companies want to adopt AI, but their processes remain fragmented across people, messages, and systems. Deployment.co starts with Jarbas, which identifies the highest-impact workflow; we then implement, measure, and operate the solution. The revenue path is explicit: 10 Group accounts at US$3k, 10 Managed accounts at US$5k, and 3 Strategic accounts at US$7k create 23 active accounts and US$101k in modeled MRR. Four launch cycles are designed to generate the required pipeline. Capital becomes relevant after demand, delivery capacity, margin, and retention prove that the constraint is execution — not market interest.”

02 • Sales thesis

Do not sell five agents. Sell an operation that works better.

What the customer buys

Fewer missed tasks, faster responses, visible processes, clear owners and context-aware decisions.

How Deployment.co delivers

Jarbas identifies the bottleneck. Deployment.co designs the workflow, connects systems, implements the necessary agents and tracks performance.

How value becomes visible

Cycle time, backlog, errors, conversion, follow-ups, human interventions and operating hours before and after.

03 • Founder pain points

The sale begins with the burden the founder feels — not the technology.

“Everything depends on me.”

The company grew, but every exception still comes back to the founder. The founder has become the bottleneck.

Message: your company must keep operating even when you are not in every conversation.

“My team works hard, but the work disappears.”

Messages, spreadsheets, CRM and tasks are disconnected. No one sees the entire process, and opportunities are forgotten.

Message: effort without a system becomes rework, delays and silent losses.

“I do not know where I am losing money.”

Customer response is slow, follow-up fails and data arrives too late to correct the month.

Message: the invisible problem keeps charging you every day.

“I bought tools, but nothing changed.”

The company accumulated software without redesigning the work, ownership or exceptions.

Message: Deployment.co connects technology to the real operating process.

“I am afraid of falling behind.”

The founder sees competitors talking about AI but does not know where to start without creating risk, cost and distraction.

Message: starting with the right workflow reduces anxiety and produces evidence.

“I cannot lose control.”

Automating decisions without clear guardrails threatens customers, cash and reputation.

Message: consequential actions begin with human approval, traceability and defined accountability.
